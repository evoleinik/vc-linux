bootLogo: turtle graphics in a real DOS COM program.

Type BOOTLOGO on VC's command line, or select BOOTLOGO.COM and press Enter.
Type one line at a time and finish each line with Enter.
QUIT exits. VC then asks for one more Enter to restore its panels.

Commands (names can be lower case):
  CLEARSCREEN       Clear the screen; center the turtle facing north.
                    Use this command alone on its line.
  FD 40 / BK 40     Move forward / backward, drawing unless the pen is up.
  RT 25 / LT 25     Turn right / left in degrees.
  REPEAT 10 FD 10   Repeat a command, or a list inside [ and ].
  PU / PD          Pen up / pen down.
  SETCOLOR 1       Pen color 0-3; 0 is the background.
  TO name ... END  Define a procedure. Only its first two letters matter.
                    Use a command or [list]; at most 120 characters.
  QUIT             Exit (use alone on its line).

This is a tiny interpreter: always close brackets, avoid zero distances
or repeat counts (zero means 65536), and keep command lines short.
Procedures cannot be redefined. CLEARSCREEN does not erase procedures.

Three programs to type, one line at a time:

1. Square
  CLEARSCREEN
  REPEAT 4 [FD 50 RT 90]

2. Star
  CLEARSCREEN
  REPEAT 36 [FD 60 RT 170]

3. Flower (from bootLogo's original README)
  CLEARSCREEN
  TO CURVE REPEAT 4 [LT 10 FD 10] END
  TO PETAL [CURVE LT 140 CURVE] END
  TO FLOWER REPEAT 4 [PETAL LT 50] END
  FLOWER
  RT 180
  FD 50
  RT 140
  PETAL
  PU
  FD 50
