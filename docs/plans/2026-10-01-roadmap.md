## Findings, 2026-10-01

**8. Roguelikes** (16-bit real-mode DOS builds only):
- **UMoria 5.6** is GPL-2 since 2008, and is the same codebase as the 5.5.2 DOS builds (Turbo C 2.0,
  MSC 5-6). DOS support was stripped only later, in 5.7 (2020). Best next candidate.
- **Hack 1.0.3** is BSD, the most permissive of the set, and was ported to MS-DOS with Microsoft C
  by Don Kneller. Small.
- **NetHack 3.0.6 to 3.3.1** had real 16-bit DOS builds under the NetHack General Public Licence,
  but they rely on an overlay manager, which is new runtime work. 3.4.3 needs DPMI, out of scope.
- Omega (GPL-2) and ULarn (GPL) are possible; Larn's licence is non-commercial, so it is out.

**9. Keen Dreams and Wolfenstein 3D:**
- **Keen Dreams:** the source is GPL-2+ (2014), but the game data was never freed and is still sold.
  It fails criterion 2 of the games doc. EGA/CGA only.
- **Wolfenstein 3D:** the source is GPL (re-licensed 2012), built with Borland C++ 3.0, 16-bit. The
  shareware episode 1 data was meant for free copying, but the exact vendor terms were not
  verified. It needs VGA mode 13h, OPL2 music and Sound Blaster digitized sound.
- **Decision for now:** park 9 until the shareware terms are read from the original `VENDOR.DOC` or
  equivalent. Do UMoria and Hack 1.0.3 for item 8 first.
